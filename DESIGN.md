# PetalPatch design direction

## Visual world

PetalPatch is a public security research index with the quiet, readable feel of a developer publication. It borrows the reference site's generous whitespace, simple navigation, and text-led entries while keeping its own name, flower mark, copy, and research workflow.

## Durable decisions

- Use a near-white or charcoal canvas, near-black or warm-white text, and one restrained violet accent for links, focus, and active controls. Keep severity colors meaningful and distinct.
- Pair DM Sans for interface and body text, Newsreader for titles, and DM Mono only for CVE identifiers and compact source metadata.
- Use a centered reading width, flat surfaces, and fine horizontal rules. Avoid ornamental cards, background gradients, constellations, and decorative grids.
- Keep the archive header compact, with recent lookups available as a quiet horizontal list. Put the primary lookup first and let its result open as a clear report with a supporting source column.
- On narrow screens, collapse the report to one column, make recent lookups horizontally browsable, and preserve comfortable tap targets.
- Keep NVD provenance, publication and reporting details, affected products, references, and the unknown discovery-domain state clear and easy to scan.
- Preserve the light/dark toggle with parallel neutral palettes, visible focus rings, reduced-motion support, and legible text selection.

## Avoid

- Reusing Sekai's logo, artwork, copy, or site-specific identity.
- Heavy cards, shadows, gradients, dashboard ornament, terminal styling, or decorative monospace text.
- Treating reference hosts or affected-product vendors as confirmed discovery or attack domains.
